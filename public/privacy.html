Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LiverQ (the “app”) handles information. LiverQ is a local-only educational study app.

No account required

LiverQ does not require an account. There is no sign-in, registration, or user profile.

No personal information collected

LiverQ does not collect personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying details.

On-device storage only

Your quiz progress (such as answered questions, incorrect answers, and bookmarks) is stored locally on your device using your browser’s localStorage. This progress stays on your device and is not transmitted to a server. Clearing your browser storage, or using the “Clear local progress” option in the app, removes it.

Do not enter patient information

Do not enter patient identifiers or protected health information (PHI) into this app. LiverQ is a study tool and is not intended to receive or store any patient data.

No analytics or tracking

No analytics or tracking are currently used. LiverQ does not use advertising, cookies for tracking, or third-party analytics services.

Feedback by email

If you voluntarily send feedback by email, we receive the information you choose to include in that email. Do not include patient identifiers or protected health information.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change in a future version (for example, if optional accounts or sync are ever added), this policy will be updated before those features are released.
